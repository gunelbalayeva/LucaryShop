### Start date: June 7, 2025
# Lucary – Bringing the Magic of Art to Your Mobile Device

Lucary is a unique e-commerce iOS application that brings the world of art and creativity to your fingertips.  
Handmade goods, decorative items, original artworks, and more — **now just a tap away!**

##  About the Project

 **Lucary** is an iOS mobile platform designed for artists, designers, and creative minds.  
Our mission is to **connect creators with a wider audience**, and to offer users **authentic, high-quality, and handcrafted products made with love and care.**

With Lucary, you can:
- Discover one-of-a-kind handmade creations  
- Add your favorite products to your cart with ease  
- Enjoy a seamless experience blending traditional and modern art  

> **Lucary – Feel the art in every touch.**
